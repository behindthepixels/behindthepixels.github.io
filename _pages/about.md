# About Me

Edward Liu is Director of Applied Deep Learning Research and Distinguished Research Scientist at NVIDIA, where he leads research in AI for real-time graphics and generative visual computing. He has helped transform real-time graphics through AI, leading key advances across the DLSS technology suite, which is now widely adopted across the industry and has become a defining RTX product differentiator. He has also helped advance the convergence of AI and real-time ray tracing in modern interactive graphics. His current work spans DLSS 5 Neural Rendering, real-time visual generation, and broader research shaping the future of AI-powered interactive visual computing. Across foundational research, productization, and developer ecosystem integration, he translates breakthroughs into practical technologies deployed at industry scale. At SIGGRAPH 2026, Liu will share NVIDIA’s vision for the next generation of AI-powered graphics.
